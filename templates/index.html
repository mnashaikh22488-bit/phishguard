Phishing Awareness

Learn the basics of phishing and how to stay safe online.

1. What is phishing?

Phishing is a social engineering attack where attackers impersonate trusted entities to steal credentials, money, or personal data.

2. How phishing works

Scammers send fake messages with urgent stories, malicious links, or attachments that lead to credential theft or malware.

3. How to identify fake links

Check the domain carefully, look for HTTPS, avoid untrusted shorteners, and never rely on the display text alone.

4. Email phishing

Fake bank, HR, or support emails often use generic greetings, urgent deadlines, and links to lookalike login pages.

5. SMS phishing (smishing)

Text messages about packages, taxes, or account locks that push you to click a link or call a fake number.

6. Social media scams

Fake giveaways, impersonation accounts, and DMs asking for codes or payments are common on social platforms.

7. Fake login pages

Pages that copy real brands but use slightly wrong domains. Always type the official site yourself when logging in.

8. OTP scams

Attackers who already have your username may ask for an OTP. Never share one-time codes with anyone.

9. Payment scams

Requests for gift cards, crypto, or urgent wire transfers are classic fraud. Verify independently before paying.

10. After clicking a suspicious link

Stop entering data, close the page, scan your device, change passwords from a safe device, and report the incident.

Top 7 Phishing Red Flags

  1. Unexpected message
  2. Extreme urgency
  3. Suspicious domain
  4. Request for password / OTP
  5. Unexpected payment request
  6. Fake rewards
  7. Unknown attachments
